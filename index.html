5MON 14Lake Louise and Moraine LakeOvernight: Canmore · the must-see day, two lakes
Fixed: timed bus reservationBook in April 2027Easy flat walks
  1. 06:15Breakfast in the suite, pack lunch
  2. ~07:00Roam Route 3 Canmore → Banff, ~25–30 minCovered by the Super Pass below.
  3. ~07:45Roam 8X Banff → Lake Louise Lakeshore, ~1 hUse the Roam Reservable Super Pass: CA$30 adult in 2026. It covers the 8X round trip, all Roam buses that day, and the Parks Canada Lake Connector between Lake Louise and Moraine Lake. Private cars cannot drive to Moraine Lake.
  4. 09:00Lake Louise shorelineFlat lakeshore trail along the water, 1–1½ h. Canoe rental at the boathouse is a splurge.
  5. 10:45Lake Connector shuttle to Moraine LakeFirst come, first served at the tent, ~20 min.
  6. 11:15Moraine Lake and the Rockpile viewpointThe Rockpile is a short climb on steps and gravel, about 10 min, to the classic Ten Peaks view. Lunch by the lake. Allow 2 h.
  7. 14:00Connector back, 8X to Banff, Route 3 to CanmoreTake the return time on your reservation. Back in Canmore ~17:15.
  8. 19:00Dinner
Meals
  • Breakfast & packed lunch: from the suite kitchen
  • Backup lunch: Laggan's Bakery in Lake Louise village
  • Dinner: back in Canmore, early night
Bookings
  • 2026 pattern: Parks Canada shuttle seats opened 15 April (40% of seats), the rest 2 days ahead at 08:00. Roam's June Super Pass dates opened 23 April. Watch for the 2027 dates in March.
Seasonal notes
  • Moraine Lake road usually opens about 1 June. In mid-June the lake may still be low or part-frozen, with snow on the peaks.
  • Mornings near freezing: bring hats and gloves.

Book gondola onlineFlexible times

  5. 14:30Banff Upper Hot SpringsA few minutes from the gondola base. CA$19.75 adult (2026). Towel and swimsuit rentals on site. 1 h is plenty.

Meals

  • Dinner: light, in the suite or takeout in Canmore

  • Cave and Basin National Historic Site is partly indoors (CA$10 adult, 2026)
7WED 16Lake Minnewanka cruise and Johnston CanyonOvernight: Canmore · two places, both by Roam bus
Book cruiseOrder can flipLight walking
  1. 08:45Route 3 to Banff, then Roam Route 6 to Lake MinnewankaRoute 6 is free with a same-day cruise ticket. Leave Canmore about 1½ h before sailing.
  2. 10:30Lake Minnewanka Classic Cruise, 1 hThe largest lake in the park, with commentary. CA$75 adult booked ahead (2026). Check the June 2027 sailing times; the 2027 calendar lists 10:00, 13:00 and 19:00 from 26 June, and earlier-June times may differ.
  3. 12:30Back to town, lunch
  4. 14:00Roam Route 9 to Johnston Canyon, ~35 minCA$5 each way (2026), departures about hourly.
  5. 14:45Johnston Canyon Lower Falls1.1–1.2 km each way, mostly paved, with catwalks bolted to the canyon wall. About 1 h return. Upper Falls adds ~1.5 km each way and more climbing; skip unless you feel fresh.
  6. 17:00Bus back to Banff, then Route 3 to Canmore
Meals
  • Breakfast: suite kitchen
  • Lunch: Nourish Bistro (vegetarian, ~CA$30)
  • Dinner: light, in the suite or takeout in Canmore
Bigger alternative
  • Icefields Parkway coach tour to the Columbia Icefield (full day)
  • Lazy option: skip the canyon, stay on Banff Avenue

10SAT 19Stanley Park seawall and Granville IslandOvernight: Vancouver · the one full city day
FlexibleFlat walking
  1. 08:00Breakfast near Bridgeport stationCafés and bakeries around Richmond Centre and No. 3 Road, or grab something downtown.
  2. 09:00Walk to Bridgeport, Canada Line downtown, ~20–25 minAbout 8 min on foot along Bridgeport Road. Buy a Compass DayPass (covers all zones).
  3. 10:00Bus toward Stanley Park
  4. 11:00Stanley Park seawall: Coal Harbour to Brockton PointFlat path past the totem poles and Lions Gate Bridge views, 1½–2 h on foot. A bike rental lets you do the full ~9 km loop (extra cost).
  5. 13:30English Bay beach, then a small ferry across False CreekAquabus or False Creek Ferries to Granville Island.
  6. 14:00Granville Island Public MarketLate lunch from the stalls, 1½–2 h.
  7. 17:00Gastown and WaterfrontShort flat walk near Waterfront station, the Canada Line's first stop.
  8. 18:30Dinner downtown, Canada Line back to Bridgeport, packIf it is dark or you are tired, take a short taxi from the station to the hotel.
Meals
  • Breakfast: café or bakery near the hotel (~CA$15)
  • Lunch: Granville Island Market stalls (~CA$18)
  • Dinner: Hokkaido Ramen Santouka (~CA$25) or Japadog (~CA$12)
Swap-in (not in the ₱200k plan)
  • Sea to Sky Gondola and Shannon Falls by Squamish Connector shuttle: about CA$125 each. It replaces the city day.
  • Capilano Suspension Bridge (~CA$80, free downtown shuttle) or free Lynn Canyon
Bad weather
  • The market is covered; skip the seawall
11SUN 20Train to Seattle, Pike Place MarketOvernight: Seattle · border crossing day
Fixed: trainBook Amtrak
  1. 06:00Taxi to Pacific Central Station, ~20–25 minBook the night before. Eat at the station or on the train. Amtrak asks for early arrival for US border inspection.
  2. ~07:25Amtrak Cascades 517 Vancouver → Seattle, arrives ~11:40–12:10Two trains a day each way in 2026: morning and late afternoon. Times shifted during 2026, so confirm the 2027 timetable. Coach fares from about US$40–85.
  3. 12:15Link light rail to SeaTac/Airport, ~35–40 minInt'l District/Chinatown station is next to King Street Station. The hotel is a 4–5 min walk from SeaTac/Airport station. Leave bags at the desk (or check in if the room is ready).
  4. 13:30Link back to Westlake, ~40 min
  5. 14:15Pike Place Market and the new waterfrontLate lunch from the stalls; 2–3 h wandering.
  6. 18:00Light dinner, Link back to the hotel
Meals
  • Breakfast: station or the train café car
  • Lunch: Pike Place Chowder (~US$18), Piroshky Piroshky (~US$10), Beecher's mac & cheese
  • Dinner: light, takeout before the ride back
Reminders
  • Carry passports, US visas and onward flight details on the train
  • Pike Place Chowder kept 11:00–17:00 hours; go early
Alternative

12MON 21Ferry to Bainbridge Island, Kerry Park viewpointOvernight: Seattle · water and mountain views, easy pace
FlexibleEasy walking

  2. 10:10Walk-on ferry from Colman Dock to Bainbridge, 35 minUS$11.35 walk-on in 2026, paid westbound only; the return is free. Stand on the outer deck for the skyline and, on clear days, Mount Rainier and the Olympic Mountains.

  6. 18:30Light dinner, Link back to the hotel
Meals

  • Dinner: light, takeout near the station
